# Jira Documentation: Creating and Managing Sprints

## What is a Sprint in Jira?
A Sprint is a short, time-boxed period (usually 1 to 4 weeks) during which a Scrum team works to complete a set amount of work from the backlog.

## How to Create a Sprint
1. Open your Jira Scrum project and go to the **Backlog** view in the sidebar.
2. At the top of the backlog, click the **Create Sprint** button.
3. A new empty sprint container will appear above your backlog items.
4. Drag and drop issues from your **Backlog** into the newly created sprint.
5. Click **Edit Sprint** (via the three dots menu on the sprint header) to configure:
   - **Sprint Name** (e.g., "Sprint 24 - Checkout Feature")
   - **Duration** (e.g., 2 weeks)
   - **Start Date** and **End Date**
   - **Sprint Goal** (e.g., "Complete payment gateway migration")
6. When the team is ready to start, click **Start Sprint** at the top right of the sprint section.
7. Confirm the dates and goal in the dialog, then click **Start**.

## Managing Active Sprints
- View progress on the **Active Sprints** / Board view.
- Drag cards across columns (`To Do`, `In Progress`, `Done`) as work advances.
- At the end of the timebox, click **Complete Sprint** to close the sprint and automatically move any incomplete issues to the next sprint or backlog.
