# Jira Documentation: Creating and Managing Epics

## What is an Epic in Jira?
An Epic in Jira Software represents a large body of work that can be broken down into several smaller issues, such as Stories, Tasks, and Bugs. Epics often span multiple sprints and multiple teams.

## How to Create an Epic
1. Log in to your Jira Software project.
2. Click the **+ Create** button in the top navigation bar.
3. In the **Issue Type** dropdown, select **Epic**.
4. Enter a clear **Summary** (e.g., "Redesign User Onboarding Flow").
5. Fill in the **Epic Name** field (a concise label used on boards and backlog views).
6. (Optional) Provide a detailed **Description**, assign an owner (**Assignee**), set a **Target Start** and **Target End** date.
7. Click **Create**.

## Linking Issues to an Epic
You can add child issues to an Epic from the Backlog or the Epic details view:
- **From the Backlog view**: Open the Epics panel on the left side of the Backlog, then drag and drop existing issues into the desired Epic.
- **From an existing Issue**: Open the issue, locate the **Epic Link** field, and select the Epic from the dropdown menu.

## Tracking Epic Progress
- On Scrum and Kanban boards, enable the Epic panel to view total issues completed versus remaining.
- Use the **Timeline** or **Roadmap** view to visualize Epic start/end dates and dependencies across sprints.
